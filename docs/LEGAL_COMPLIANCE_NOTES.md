# Legal and compliance notes for future RF work

This repo does not perform live RF interception. It uses local AIS CSV files and synthetic RF simulation.

For future live RF phases:

- Keep architecture metadata-only.
- Do not record or transcribe VHF voice content.
- Do not collect satphone content.
- Confirm ACMA licensing/receive-equipment requirements before deployment.
- Confirm Telecommunications (Interception and Access) Act exposure before collecting any communications metadata or content.
- Use lawful customer-side basis for any government deployment.
- Keep all simulated demos clearly watermarked.

This is not legal advice. Get specialist review before field collection.
